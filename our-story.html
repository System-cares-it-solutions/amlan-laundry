Our Story

More Than a Laundry Service

The Salavai Laundry started with a simple belief: the laundry industry, one of the oldest and most essential trades in India, deserved better. Better tools. Better training. Better livelihoods for the people who have been washing our clothes for generations.

We built The Salavai Laundry to bring structure, technology and dignity to this work, and to open doors for people ready to build a livelihood in it. Every store we open trains and employs people from the local community. Every machine we bring in makes their work lighter and their income steadier. Every order you place keeps that momentum going.

This store is where that vision meets your everyday life. It is not charity. It is a laundry service built on a better foundation, one where quality and purpose grow together.

You're Not Just a Customer. You're Part of the Movement.

Every time you book with The Salavai Laundry, you are doing two things at once. You are getting your clothes cleaned by people who know what they are doing. And you are backing a growing network of laundry entrepreneurs, workers and communities across South India who are building better lives through this trade.

No extra steps. No add-on donations. Just choose us for your laundry, and you become part of a bigger story, one wash at a time.
