SIGP

Self Income Generating Program

The Self Income Generating Program (SIGP) is our social initiative dedicated to creating sustainable livelihood opportunities through the laundry industry. We empower dhobis, homemakers, persons with disabilities, and aspiring entrepreneurs by helping them establish small-scale laundry ventures with minimal investment. Through practical training, operational guidance, equipment support, and ongoing mentorship, SIGP enables individuals to build a stable source of income with confidence and dignity. Our mission is to transform livelihoods by making entrepreneurship accessible, fostering self-reliance, and creating lasting economic opportunities for underserved communities.

BPP

Business Partner Program

The Business Partner Program (BPP) is designed for aspiring entrepreneurs who want to build a successful laundry enterprise with the backing of an experienced team. We provide complete end-to-end support, including location planning, store design, equipment procurement, installation, staff training, operational systems, branding, marketing, and after-sales assistance. Our proven processes and continuous business guidance help partners establish, operate, and scale profitable laundry stores with confidence. BPP offers the expertise, infrastructure, and long-term support needed to build a thriving and sustainable laundry enterprise under The Salavai Laundry brand.
