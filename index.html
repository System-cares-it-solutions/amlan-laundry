Our Equipment & Technology

Modern Machines. Gentler on Every Fabric.

With State-of-art infrastructure, The Salavai Laundry runs on world class commercial laundry equipment, trusted by leading hospitality and garment-care businesses. Precise temperature control, calibrated wash cycles and consistent drying mean every load gets the same high standard of care, whether it's your bedsheets or your best silk saree.

LG Wet Cleaning Machine: A Cleaner Way to Dry Clean

Traditional dry cleaning relies on chemical solvents that can be harsh on fabric and on the environment. The LG Wet Cleaning Machine offers a smarter alternative. It uses water-based, biodegradable cleaning agents combined with precise drum motion and moisture control to clean delicate fabrics as thoroughly as dry cleaning, without the harsh chemicals resulting in a garment care process that protects colour, texture and shape, while being significantly kinder to the environment. We deliver premium cleaning that you and the planet can feel good about.

Kleider Care: Chemicals Engineered for Fabric, Not Against It

Every wash at The Salavai Laundry uses specialised detergents and cleaning agents from Kleider Care, formulated specifically for professional laundry use. These are engineered to deliver maximum cleaning performance while causing zero damage to fabric fibres, colour or finish. Kleider Care solutions are also designed with environmental safety in mind, so what's tough on stains stays gentle on the planet. It's how we deliver spotless, fresh results without compromising on fabric health or sustainability.

Everything you need to know about our store services, door-to-door pickup, and fabric care

You can easily schedule a pickup by filling out our online booking form or messaging us on WhatsApp (+91 6385550203). Our executive will visit your address, weigh or count your garments, provide an immediate digital receipt, and return your clean, freshly pressed clothes back to your doorstep.
