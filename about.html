About The Amlan Laundry

Our Story

When we entered the laundry industry, we saw a rapidly growing market with immense potential, one that lacked structured systems, standardized processes, and dependable after-sales support. Rather than following the conventional approach, we chose to build a strong foundation from the ground up.

We partnered with leading laundry equipment manufacturers known for their high-performance machines and established our own technical team to deliver end-to-end project execution, including planning, installation, commissioning, and after-sales service. Over the past two years, we have successfully completed more than 1000 laundry setups across India, working closely with multiple vendors and gaining valuable industry expertise.

Industry Insights

The Gap We Identified

Our experience revealed that many laundry businesses and franchise models struggle because they lack standardized operating procedures, proper training, and ongoing business support. To address this challenge, we partnered with industry professionals who have over a decade of hands-on experience, enabling us to create a reliable ecosystem for technical support, operational excellence, and continuous business guidance.

Live Training & Incubation Centre

The Amlan Laundry, Chennai

This vision led to the launch of The Amlan Laundry in Chennai, a live operating laundry facility that serves two purposes. First, it delivers premium retail laundry services to customers. Second, it functions as a practical training and incubation center where aspiring entrepreneurs gain real-world experience in every aspect of running a successful laundry business.

Today, we empower entrepreneurs to establish profitable and sustainable laundry businesses under The Amlan Laundry brand, backed by proven systems, comprehensive training, reliable equipment, and dedicated after-sales support. We are building a new benchmark for the organized laundry industry in India.
